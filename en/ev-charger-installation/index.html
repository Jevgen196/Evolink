  • Installation paperwork, and an explanation of the formalities that apply in your case.

Why the RCD matters

Every charge point has its own 30 mA RCD. A type AC RCD is not valid for charging an electric car: it cannot detect the DC leakage that the charger and car electronics can produce. If your charger already has a type AC RCD, it can be replaced with type A or HI.

FAQ

How much power do I need to contract?

A single-phase 7.4 kW (32 A) charger needs that power free on top of the home's consumption. If your contract falls short, the charging current can be limited or dynamic power management used. We check it against your panel and contract.

What cable size is used?

It is calculated from the distance and the current. For a single-phase 32 A charger it is usually 6 mm² copper over normal distances; long runs may need more. I confirm it on site.

Do I need a new meter?

In a single home, normally not: the charger connects to the existing installation. A separate meter only comes up in specific cases (for example a community garage with no pre-installation).

I live in an apartment building. Can I install one?

Spain's Horizontal Property Act (art. 17.5) lets you install a charge point in your own parking space without a vote of the owners' association; you must notify the community beforehand. I help with the technical details they ask for.

How long does it take?

A normal installation takes one day. Long conduit runs or common areas can take longer.
